# Requirements (seed)

Implement **customer notification preferences**.

- A customer can view current email notification preference.
- A customer can enable or disable email notifications for themselves.
- The system must not send an email notification when the customer has opted out.
- Preference is owned by the customer record, not by the notification sender.
